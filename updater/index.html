Why USB? The firmware is written straight to the chip’s flash in ROM download mode, so it works even when the receiver’s firmware or Bluetooth is unresponsive — it is also faster, and the result is read back and verified afterwards. Bluetooth update is the convenient option when the receiver is already paired and running.
